# Privacy

## v1 promise

- **No login required** for the library.
- Official Grok export files are parsed **in your browser**.
- Conversations are stored in **this device’s IndexedDB**.
- We do not need your chats to host the marketing site.

## Data we do not collect in the library flow

- Conversation contents  
- Export ZIP/JSON contents  
- Thoughts / thinking traces  

Those stay on your device unless **you** export or share them.

## Hosting notes

Any deployed website may produce standard host logs (IP, user agent, URL paths). That is separate from conversation storage.

## Official Grok

Account export is provided by Grok (xAI) under their policies. This product only helps you **read and keep** files you already can download.

## Children

Not directed at children. Use only if you can lawfully use Grok and store your own data.

## Contact / changes

Privacy posture may expand if optional sync (v2) ships—always opt-in and explained first.
